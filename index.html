KOULA – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KOULA ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use the KOULA mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use KOULA, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Information: name, email address, phone number, physical address
  • Financial Information: payment information
  • Location Information: precise location and approximate/coarse location
  • User Content: photos and videos, other user content, emails or text messages
  • Identifiers: user ID
  • Purchases: purchase history
  • Usage Data: product interaction and other usage data
  • Diagnostics: crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use information to create and manage accounts, allow users to create and book services, process payments and transactions, facilitate communication, provide location-based functionality, provide customer support, and maintain and improve KOULA.

We may also use certain information for analytics, troubleshooting, security, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

3. Payments

Payments made through KOULA may be processed by third-party payment service providers. Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies. KOULA does not necessarily store complete payment card information on its own systems.

4. Location Information

KOULA may request access to precise or approximate location information to show nearby services, help users find suitable service providers, and enable location-based features. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

5. Photos and User Content

Users may voluntarily upload photos, videos, messages, descriptions, or other content to KOULA. This content may be processed and displayed to other users when necessary to provide KOULA's services.

6. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate KOULA, including payment processors, cloud hosting providers, analytics and diagnostic providers, communication providers, and other technical service providers.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of KOULA, our users, or others.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain appropriate business records. When information is no longer necessary, it may be deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, or object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

9. Children's Privacy

KOULA is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Third-Party Services

KOULA may use third-party services for functionality, payments, analytics, hosting, diagnostics, notifications, and other technical purposes. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date indicates when the policy was most recently updated.
